# Build Log

Keep entries short and dated. The goal is to capture what you worked on,
any problems you ran into, and how you solved (or are trying to solve)
them. This becomes your source material for resume bullets, interview
answers, and just remembering what past-you was thinking.

Tip: write the entry as soon as you hit a problem or fix it — details
fade fast, and "I fixed a wiring issue" is much less useful later than
"servo 3 was jittering because it shared a ground with the mux, fixed
by adding a separate ground rail."
